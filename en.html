RoutineCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

RoutineCue ("the app") is a voice-guided routine timer. It is built on a privacy-first design principle.

1. Information We Collect

The app does not collect or transmit any user data, including personal information.

  • There is no account registration and no input field for names, email addresses, or similar data.
  • Your routines and tasks are stored only on your device.
  • The app does not communicate with external servers (except for in-app purchases, below).
  • The app contains no third-party SDKs for ads, analytics, or crash reporting.

2. In-App Purchases

The one-time Pro purchase is processed by Google Play's billing system. Payment information is handled by Google; the app and its developer never access it. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications: used to show routine progress while running.
  • Alarms: used to auto-start routines at the time you set.

These permissions are never used to send data anywhere.

4. Data Deletion

All data lives only on your device. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage from system settings) deletes it completely.

6. Changes
